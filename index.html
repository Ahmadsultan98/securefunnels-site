FAQs

Common Questions

What exactly is a DMCA takedown? +
A DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) takedown is a formal legal notice sent to platforms hosting your copyrighted content without permission. Platforms are legally required to remove the content once they receive a valid notice. We handle the entire process on your behalf, drafting, filing, and following up until removal is confirmed.
How fast can you remove pirated content? +
Our average removal time is 7 days from when we file the notice. For major platforms like Google, YouTube, and most file sharing sites, removal typically happens within 3 to 5 business days. Some platforms take longer but we follow up persistently until every link is gone.
Do I need to provide legal documents? +
You simply need to confirm you own the content. Usually a screenshot of your original upload date, your course platform profile, or a signed declaration is sufficient. We guide you through exactly what is needed for each case, it takes 5 minutes on your end.
What types of content can you protect? +
Online courses, eBooks, videos, photos, software, music, podcasts, written content, and more. If you created it and someone else is distributing it without permission, we can help get it removed.
How does the B2B Lead Generation service work? +
You tell us who you sell to. We find companies showing buying intent, pick the right decision makers, verify their contact details, and deliver a clean sheet ready for your CRM or outreach tool. If you want, we can also write and run the email outreach for you. Every new client starts with a free sample of 50 leads.
What is included in the free piracy audit? +
The audit is normally $149 and is free for new clients. We scan marketplaces, Google and social media for copies of your content, fake listings and copycat sellers, then send you a clear report so you can see the problem before you pay anything.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment? +
No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. We are confident in our results and believe clients stay because of the value we deliver, not because they are locked in.
